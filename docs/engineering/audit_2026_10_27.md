## Confirmed implementation limitations

- baselines._extract_feature_vector silently imputes missing numbers to zero;
  boolean coercion accepts malformed values. Target joins keep first duplicate
  dataset index without checking prompt identity or strict eligibility.
- load_hidden_states skips malformed files. Historical construction scripts expect
  hidden_states/last_layer_mean_pooled keys absent from the inspected raw payload.
  Therefore those scripts alone do not reproduce the saved X_hidden artifact.
- Existing README and experiment.yaml describe an earlier phase and obsolete labels.
- cv_run_proper.py fits scaler/PCA within training folds, but the nested-CV runner
  that produced expanded_model_selection results was not located. Inner selection,
  resumed search changes and full preprocessing boundaries cannot be certified
  from saved predictions. Report provenance gaps, not confirmed leakage.
- No usable production inference contract, validated calibration, individual
  uncertainty interval, or validated routing thresholds was found.

## Scientific limits and remaining checks

Prompt-ID separation does not prove separation by shared SQuAD context/topic or
near-duplicate content. Full raw-content duplication, all hidden rows, evaluator
record reconciliation and every prompt feature value remain to be checked.
There is no confirmed use of answers/evaluator labels in the five selected prompt
features or captured forward states. Generation records do contain post-generation
fields and must never be accepted as inference feature dictionaries.

Labels are evaluator judgments, not independently established correctness. Strict
exclusion can induce selection bias; ten stochastic attempts give noisy risk
targets and may be correlated. Wilson intervals on label counts are not uncertainty
intervals for a predictor. Zero-heavy targets reward a zero median under MAE while
offering no discrimination. A mean baseline estimates cohort prevalence, not
prompt-specific risk. Neither baseline is proven transferable or calibrated.
Evidence covers one frozen model/configuration and SQuAD cohort only. No external,
temporal, cross-model, or operational reliability improvement is established.
